How do I play?

Tilt your phone left and right to steer. Touch and hold the screen to fire the lantern. Land on a dead star to light it: it launches you upward and adds light to your lantern. The Dark rises from below; the run ends when it catches you.

What is stardust for?

Stardust is earned by playing and is spent in the Shop on suits, light colors and sky themes.

I bought Master's Lantern but it is not unlocked

Open Shop, go to the Master tab and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.
