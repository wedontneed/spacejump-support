Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Space Jump: Last Lamplighter contains no ads and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.

Gameplay statistics

To balance and improve the game, the app sends gameplay statistics to our own server (games.nodewires.com): when the app is opened and for how long, when a run starts and how it ends (height, score, stars, constellations and stardust), which constellations are awakened, which suit, light and theme are used, what is bought with stardust in the shop, whether the Master's Lantern purchase was started, completed or cancelled, and whether the leaderboard was opened. Each report also carries the app version, iOS version, device family (iPhone or iPad) and device language.

These records are tied to a random identifier created on your device for this app. It is not your name, Apple Account, Game Center identity or an advertising identifier, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Only the developer can see the statistics, and only as totals.

You can turn this off at any time on the main menu with Anonymous stats: Off. Statistics waiting to be sent are deleted and nothing further is sent.

Individual records are deleted within 90 days. Daily totals and the date an identifier was first seen can be kept for up to 730 days. To ask for deletion, email the address below.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, stardust balance, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive any personal information.

Purchases

The optional Master's Lantern purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.
